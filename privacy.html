What this version of the site collects

The current planning tool runs entirely in your browser. The answers you enter on the "Plan My Trip" form (destination, days, budget, interests, pace, and walking tolerance) are stored only in your browser's local storage, so your itinerary is still there if you come back to the results page. Nothing is sent to a server, and no account or personal identifying information is required to use the planner.

The contact form on this site is a visual prototype — messages are not yet transmitted or stored anywhere.

What a future, booking-enabled version would need

Once real bookings and payments are introduced, this policy will need to cover: what personal and payment information is collected, how it's stored and secured, which third parties (airlines, hotels, payment processors) it's shared with, how long it's retained, and how users can access, correct, or delete it.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember your trip answers and language preference between pages. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used in this version.
