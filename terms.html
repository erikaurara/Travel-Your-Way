What this version of the service is

Travel Your Way, in its current form, is a trip-planning tool only. It generates suggested itineraries, effort estimates, and alternative "rain plan" activities based on information you provide. It does not book, reserve, or pay for flights, accommodation, tours, or any other travel service.

No guarantee of accuracy

Suggested itineraries — including spending estimates, walking levels, travel times, and destination information — are generated automatically and are estimates only. Opening hours, prices, availability, and conditions change. Always verify details directly with venues, operators, and official sources before relying on them, especially for destinations outside our curated guides.
