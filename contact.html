Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Planning a trip doesn't require signing up. That may change once booking is added, but planning stays free and open.

Can I actually book any of this?

Not yet. This version focuses entirely on planning — the itinerary, the effort score, and the Plan B. Booking and payment are planned as a later step.

What happens if I type a destination you don't have a guide for?

We still build you a trip. It's generated from your interests, pace, and walking tolerance rather than a hand-written local guide, so it's a good starting structure — just double-check specifics like opening hours before you go.

How is the Effort Score calculated?

It combines your chosen travel style, how much walking each day involves, and how many long transfers are in the itinerary. Tapping "Make My Trip Easier" recalculates it with lighter pacing and fewer transfers.

Is my trip data saved anywhere?

Right now, everything lives only in your browser (nothing is sent to a server). Closing your browser or clearing site data will clear it too.
