About us

Most itineraries assume everyone travels the same way

We didn't think that was true, so we built the trip planner around the traveler first and the destination second.

Where this started

Most trip guides give everyone the same list: the top ten sights, in the same order, regardless of whether you're the type to wake up at 5am for a hike or the type who needs a slow coffee before doing anything at all. We kept ending up with itineraries that looked great on paper and exhausted us by day three — or itineraries so relaxed we felt like we'd skipped the trip entirely.

So we started asking a different first question. Not "where do you want to go," but "how do you want to feel while you're there." Everything else — the stops, the pacing, the backup plans — gets built around that answer.

What makes a trip "ours"

Two things we haven't seen done well elsewhere:

  • An honest effort score. Every trip we build comes with a plain-language read on how much walking, transferring, and early-morning starting it actually involves — before you commit to it, not after you're three days in.
  • A Plan B for every day. Weather and closures are normal, not exceptions. Every day in your itinerary already has an indoor alternative ready to go.

Where we are today

This is an early, planning-only version of Travel Your Way. There's no booking or payment yet — the focus right now is getting the itinerary and the pacing right. Real bookings are the next step, once the planning experience itself earns your trust.
